This policy covers rampjet.ai. RAMP/JET LLC, a Tennessee limited liability company doing business as RampJet ("RampJet", "we", "us"), runs this site. We keep this short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you type it into a form on this site, for example to download a research brief or to request a baseline. We also record which form you used, so we know what you asked for.
  • Anything else you choose to send us, such as the contents of an email you write to us.
  • Basic usage data through Google Analytics: pages viewed, roughly where visits come from, device and browser type, and events such as a form being submitted. Google Analytics uses cookies to do this. We use it to see which pages are useful, not to identify you.

How we use it

  • To deliver what you asked for, such as the research brief.
  • To send you occasional emails: new research, our newsletter, and news about RampJet's services. You agree to these when you submit a form that says so, and you can stop them at any time.
  • To reply when you contact us.

Unsubscribing

Every marketing email we send includes an unsubscribe link. You can also reply "unsubscribe" to any of our emails and we will remove you.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the services that run this site for us:

  • Formspree (formspree.io) receives and stores form submissions for us.
  • Google Analytics (Google) measures site usage.
  • Our email inbox: Formspree forwards each submission to our company email, where we keep the list for now. If we move to a newsletter service, we will name it here before using it.

These providers may process data in the United States. We may also disclose information if the law requires it.

How long we keep it

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Analytics data is kept according to our Google Analytics retention settings.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you by replying to any email from us or by sending a message through the contact form on our homepage. If you are in the EU or UK, we rely on your consent to send marketing email, and you can withdraw it at any time. You can also block analytics cookies in your browser settings.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update it on this page and change the date at the top.
